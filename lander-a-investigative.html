This is not a scare piece. Every claim in this report links directly to the FBI, US Department of Justice, US Treasury, or the FTC. What we found is genuinely alarming — and every American timeshare owner should know about it.

The American timeshare industry has spawned one of the most destructive consumer fraud ecosystems in the country. Behind the glossy brochures lies a landscape of coercive sales tactics, inescapable contracts, fraudulent exit companies, and — in the most alarming development — direct involvement by criminal organizations officially designated as Foreign Terrorist Organisations by the US government.

The Purchase Trap: How Millions Got Locked In

Timeshare presentations across Las Vegas, Florida, and Mexico are advertised as "90-minute" commitments in exchange for free meals or theme park tickets. Consumer protection attorneys have documented sessions exceeding 10 hours — with buyers denied food, water, and rest.

"In many cases, people have been kept in a room for over 10 hours, not given water, not eaten, they are exhausted and sign contracts just to get out. At least once a month someone comes to us who owes over $150,000 in timeshare debt."

Consumer Protection Attorney — Kiplinger

Salespeople rotate in fresh shifts while the buyer grows exhausted. Tactics include fabricated scarcity, invented time limits, and false investment claims. The FTC has confirmed sellers routinely provide incomplete information about contract terms. 📎 FTC Source

Maintenance Fees: The Endless Escalator

One documented case: a family purchased a timeshare for $20,000 with an annual fee of $800. Within five years, the fee climbed to $1,500/year. When they tried to sell, the best offer was $2,000 for the entire property. Special assessments can add thousands more without warning. 📎 Fraud.org

Contracts Designed to Be Inescapable

Most American timeshare contracts include perpetuity clauses that bind the owner for their entire lifetime — with no exit option. Worse: many contracts are written to pass obligations to your children and grandchildren after you die.

"This is complete nonsense — timeshares are never a financial investment. They are a bottomless pit into which you pour money forever, and most have no resale value."

Consumer Protection Attorney — Kiplinger

The Cartel Connection: The FBI Warning You Haven't Seen

The most alarming dimension of the crisis is the confirmed involvement of Mexican drug cartels — including organisations officially designated as Foreign Terrorist Organisations by the US government. 📎 FBI Official Warning

The Cartel de Jalisco Nueva Generación (CJNG) — described by the DOJ as "one of the five most dangerous transnational criminal organisations in the world" — has been running timeshare fraud operations since 2012. In February 2026, the US Treasury sanctioned an entire network of individuals and companies linked to these operations. 📎 US Treasury

"CJNG uses extreme violence and intimidation to control the timeshare network, which often targets elder U.S. citizens and can defraud victims of their life savings."

US Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen

The scheme functions as what Treasury called a "vertically integrated fraud factory": cartel-controlled resorts generate victim databases that feed boiler-room call centres staffed by English speakers posing as US brokers, attorneys, and even US Treasury Department officials.

In 2024 alone, the FBI received 900 complaints totaling over $50 million in losses — from Mexico scams alone. FinCEN flagged approximately $330 million in suspicious transactions involving Americans wiring money to scammers. 📎 CBS News

Exit Scams: The Second Wave of Victimization

Desperate to escape, owners become targets for a second predatory industry — fraudulent exit companies. In 2022, the FTC sued Consumer Law Protection LLC for $90 million in alleged fraud. Victims were told to act immediately or face "being stuck forever — with your children inheriting the burden." They paid $5,000–$30,000 upfront and received nothing. 📎 Minnesota AG

In another case, FBI Los Angeles arrested two men who stole $4.5 million from 370 elderly victims, using their Social Security income, retirement accounts, and life savings. Proceeds went to personal luxury goods. 📎 FBI Press Release

FBI Red Flags — What Federal Agencies Say to Watch For
  • Unsolicited contact claiming a "ready buyer" for your timeshare
  • Demands for upfront fees before services are rendered
  • Extreme time pressure: "this offer expires today"
  • Claims of government affiliation
  • Exit companies advising you to stop paying maintenance fees
  • Exit companies charging $5,000–$30,000 with "guaranteed" cancellation
  • Requests for wire transfers to foreign bank accounts

The Inheritance Curse: Debt That Outlives the Owner

Perhaps the most disturbing element: many timeshare contracts carry perpetuity clauses that automatically transfer ownership — and all financial obligations — to a deceased owner's heirs. Children and grandchildren receive maintenance fee demands, collection notices, and legal threats for a property they never visited and never agreed to own.

Resort companies typically refuse buybacks. The resale market is functionally dead — properties routinely list for $1 on eBay with no buyers. 📎 ACA Group
